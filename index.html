FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Do I need internet to use CHMS?

No. CHMS runs entirely on your local network. Internet is only used briefly to verify your licence — under 1 KB, every 30 days on lifetime/yearly, every 7 days on monthly.

How many devices can connect?

Unlimited. One PC runs the server. Every receptionist, lab technician, or doctor connects over your hospital Wi-Fi from any phone, tablet, or PC.

Is my data safe?

Your patient data never leaves your PC. We have no access to it. Each day is stored in its own separate database file for easy backups.

What if my server PC breaks?

Contact us. We transfer your licence to a new PC at no cost (once per year). Your data files can be copied over too.

Can I try before buying?

Yes. We offer a 3-hour free trial with all features unlocked. Contact us on WhatsApp and we'll send the trial file. No credit card, no emails, no spam.

System requirements?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), 4 GB RAM, 500 MB free disk space, and a Wi-Fi or wired LAN. A 58mm thermal printer is optional.
